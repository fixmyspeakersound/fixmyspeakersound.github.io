Earbud Sounds Quiet on One Side? Check for Wax Before You Replace It

When one earbud gets noticeably quieter than the other, the first instinct is that it has failed and needs replacing. In practice, the most common cause is far less dramatic: a thin layer of earwax and skin oil clogging the fine mesh over the speaker. The sound is still there, it just cannot get out. Here is how to tell the difference and fix it without damaging anything.

Signs it is a blocked mesh

  • The volume dropped gradually over weeks rather than suddenly.
  • The quiet bud sounds dull or muffled, with less treble, but not distorted.
  • Pressing it more firmly into your ear does not help much.
  • You can see a yellowish or grey film on the mesh when you look at it under good light.

Signs it is really a hardware problem

  • The sound cut out suddenly, or crackles and drops in and out.
  • The bud is silent even after cleaning.
  • Only one bud connects, or the balance slider in your phone's accessibility settings is off-centre.

Always check the balance setting first. On both iPhone and Android it sits under accessibility audio options, and a slider nudged to one side is an easy thing to miss.

How to clean the mesh safely

  1. Remove any silicone tips and wash those separately in warm soapy water; let them dry fully.
  2. Hold the bud with the mesh facing down so loosened debris falls out rather than in.
  3. Brush the mesh gently with a dry, soft toothbrush in small circles.
  4. For stubborn residue, use a tiny piece of reusable putty pressed lightly on the mesh and lifted away. Do not push.
  5. Wipe the outer shell with a barely damp cloth, never the mesh itself.

Avoid needles, toothpicks, and liquids poured on the mesh. They push wax deeper or reach the driver.

Finish with a gentle vibration routine

After a dry clean, playing low tones at moderate volume through the earbuds can help shake out fine particles stuck in the mesh. A free earbud and AirPods cleaning tone does this from the browser in about a minute. Compare both sides afterwards with the same song at the same volume.

Keep it from coming back

  • Wipe the buds with a dry microfibre cloth once a week.
  • Store them in their case, not loose in a pocket.
  • Replace worn silicone tips every few months; they collect residue faster as they age.
