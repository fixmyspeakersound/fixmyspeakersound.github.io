Keeping a Family Tablet's Speakers Clear

A tablet that the whole family shares lives a harder life than a personal phone. It gets used at the dinner table, in the car, on the sofa and in bed. Over a few months the speakers often start to sound quieter or slightly fuzzy, and the volume slider creeps higher and higher. In most cases nothing is broken. The speaker openings are simply getting blocked.

Where tablet speakers hide

Most tablets have speaker slots along the short edges, sometimes on both ends for stereo sound. They sit exactly where small hands hold the device, which is why they collect so much: crumbs, dried juice, dust from bags, and fluff from blankets. A thick protective case can also partly cover the slots if its cut-outs do not line up well.

Signs the speakers are blocked rather than broken

  • Sound is louder with the case removed.
  • One side sounds weaker than the other when the tablet is held in landscape.
  • Headphones sound completely normal.
  • You can see residue in the slots under a bright light.

A five-minute cleaning routine

  1. Turn the tablet off and take it out of its case.
  2. Brush along each speaker slot with a soft, dry toothbrush, sweeping across the slots rather than pushing into them.
  3. For sticky residue on the outside edge, use a barely damp microfiber cloth, then dry it straight away. Keep liquid away from the openings.
  4. Clean the case cut-outs too. Crumbs often pack into the case itself.
  5. Turn the tablet on and play a low cleaning tone with the speaker edge pointing down to shake out loosened particles. Many Galaxy tablets share the same speaker design as Samsung phones, and the Samsung speaker cleaner on Fix My Speaker works for them in the browser.

Avoid toothpicks, pins and compressed air held close to the slots. They push debris deeper or can damage the thin mesh behind the opening.

Habits that prevent it

  • Choose a case whose speaker cut-outs are wide and line up exactly with the slots.
  • Agree on a no-snacks rule while holding the tablet, or at least wipe hands first.
  • Do the quick brush routine once a month, or whenever the volume seems lower than it used to be.
  • Keep the default volume at a moderate level, which is also kinder to young ears.

When it is time for a repair

If sound stays distorted with the case off, after cleaning, and at moderate volume, or if one speaker is completely silent, the speaker or its connection may be damaged. At that point, a repair shop is the right next step.
